# FocusFlow vs. the competition

Target price: **$4.99, one-time**. No subscription, no account.

## Nearest competitors
| App / site | Price | What reviewers complain about |
|---|---|---|
| **Pomofocus.io** (nearest, a browser Pomodoro site) | Free, with a paid Premium tier | Ads/upsells; the best reports and features sit behind Premium |
| **Forest** | ~$3.99 on iOS, plus in-app purchases | Few interval choices; leaving the app kills your tree, which hurts anyone who works on their phone; stats are limited unless you pay |
| **Focus Keeper** | Free, plus Pro in-app purchases | Classic 25/5 only unless you pay; mobile only |
| **Be Focused** | Free, plus Pro | Apple only: no web, no Android |
| **Session** | Subscription | Apple only, and it costs money every month |

## Why FocusFlow is better
- **Pay once.** $4.99 one time, with no subscription or Premium tier. Every feature is included.
- **Works everywhere.** It's a single web page, so it runs on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android and iOS. That covers the platforms Be Focused and Session leave out.
- **Private.** It has no account, no tracking and no ads. All data stays on your device.
- **Fully customizable.** You set the focus, short-break and long-break lengths and a daily goal, all free. Focus Keeper charges for that.
- **No punishment for using your device.** Switching tabs doesn't fail your session the way leaving Forest kills your tree, and the timer stays accurate in the background.
- **Task tracking.** You label each session and see your focus minutes per task for the day.
- **Polished experience.** It has an animated progress ring, a real chime, desktop notifications, a countdown in the tab title, an optional auto-start for the next session, and keyboard shortcuts (Space / R / S).
- **Your data is yours.** Streaks, a 7-day chart and CSV export are free.

## Fixes made from the review research (v1.1)
- Added a per-session task label and a "today by task" breakdown
- Swapped the silent alert for a real chime, which you can turn off
- Added an auto-start option and keyboard shortcuts
- Added an animated progress ring and smoother buttons
- Fixed a bug where skipping a session counted it as completed
- Days now use your local time, not UTC, so your streak resets at your midnight
